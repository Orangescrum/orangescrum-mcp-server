# Report templates

## Weekly status (default)

```md
# <Project> status: <start date> to <end date>

**Overall:** On track | At risk | Off track. <one sentence why>

## Highlights
- <completed item that matters to stakeholders>

## In progress
- <item> (<owner>, due <date>)

## Overdue and at risk
- <item> (<owner>, was due <date>): <what is blocking it>

## Sprint
<sprint name>, <start> to <end>: <done> of <total> tasks done.

## Time logged
<hours> hours this period. Top contributors: <name> (<hours>), <name> (<hours>).

## Next week
- <planned item>
```

## Executive summary

```md
**<Project>: <On track | At risk | Off track>**
- Done this week: <count> tasks, including <top item>
- Open: <count>, of which <count> overdue
- Biggest risk: <one line>
- Decision needed: <one line, or "none">
```

## Sprint review

```md
# <Sprint name> review

Goal: <sprint goal>
Result: <done>/<total> tasks completed (<percent>%)

## Completed
## Carried over
## Notes for the next sprint
```
